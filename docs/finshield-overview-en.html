02 / Security controls

Four safety areas, two complementary defenses

Four safety areas cover mainstream Agents. Two defenses combine permission enforcement with proactive guidance and constructive advice.

Safety areaPassive defense: permissionsActive defense: guidance and advice
PrivacyRestrict camera, microphone and screen accessUse task-relevant information within privacy limits
InteractionRestrict or isolate windows, input and clipboard useUse approved tools and reduce disruption
SystemLimit system changes, process access and resourcesExplain limits; discourage repeated bypass attempts
DataControl file access, networking and sensitive-data egressUse approved data, services and processing paths
Control operations, guide behaviorPermission enforcement maintains boundaries; active defense helps Agents continue within them.

Coverage depends on the platform, integration and policy. Guidance does not grant permissions or cancel enforcement.
